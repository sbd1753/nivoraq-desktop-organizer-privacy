NIVORAQ - Desktop Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 | Version 1.0 | Applies worldwide

1. Who we are

NIVORAQ - Desktop Organizer is published under the brand NIVORAQ TECH by an individual developer. Contact: nivoraqtech@outlook.com.

2. The core principle

Your data stays on your computer. The app has no user accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry, no usage statistics, no advertising or tracking, and no automatic error or crash reports. It does not connect to the internet, and the publisher receives nothing from it.

3. What the app reads on your computer

To do its job, and only on your device, the app reads: the names, sizes and dates of items on your desktop and the targets of shortcuts; records of installed games and applications (Windows registry, Start menu, the list of your installed packaged apps, game launcher files); and the programs registered to start with Windows. File contents are not collected. These lists are shown to you and are not sent anywhere.

4. What the app stores

Only on your computer, in the app's local data folder: your settings, the time and size of the last scan, the history of organize operations (file paths, sizes, dates) so they can be undone, records of folder icons, and your own icons if you add any. The Microsoft Store version only shows startup entries and does not change them. The standalone version can turn off your own startup entries; it keeps a local backup so they can be restored.

5. Changes to your files

Files and shortcuts are moved only after you confirm. The app does not delete your files and does not overwrite files with the same name. If you ask and confirm, it creates desktop shortcuts for games that have none; you can remove those shortcuts (only while unchanged) from History.

6. Links and support e-mail (voluntary)

The About screen shows nivoraqtech@outlook.com and a link to another app of the publisher in the Microsoft Store. Only when you click them does Windows open your e-mail app or the Microsoft Store. If you write to us, the publisher receives your e-mail address, your text and any attachment you add, uses them only to answer you, handles them through a Microsoft Outlook mailbox and deletes them after up to 12 months.

7. Third parties

No data is sold or shared. If you obtain the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the download, updates and any purchase under its own terms and privacy statement. Windows itself (for example reputation checks) may contact Microsoft; the app does not control this. If your desktop is synchronized by a service such as OneDrive, that service may synchronize files you move.

8. Retention and uninstalling

Local data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes the app's local data, including the operation history; files you organized stay where they are.

9. Your rights

The data stays on your device, so you control it directly. For personal data the publisher actually holds (support e-mails) you may, depending on where you live, have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and a right to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

11. Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new version number and date.
